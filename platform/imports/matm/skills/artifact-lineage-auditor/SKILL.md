# Artifact Lineage Auditor Skill

## Purpose
Maintain trustworthy artifact lineage from source evidence to generated outputs.

## Inputs
- independent_reviews/
- exports_for_manual/
- release evidence and archive artifacts

## Procedure
1. Verify provenance metadata and lineage chain completeness.
2. Check deterministic naming and archival retention consistency.
3. Detect orphaned, duplicate, or untraceable artifacts.
4. Emit cleanup and remediation recommendations.

## Outputs
- Artifact lineage status summary.
- Archive hygiene and retention corrections.
