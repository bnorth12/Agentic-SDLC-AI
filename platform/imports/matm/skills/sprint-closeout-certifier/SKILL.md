# Sprint Closeout Certifier Skill

## Purpose
Ensure sprint closure is auditable, complete, and suitable for portfolio progression.

## Inputs
- sprint plans, delivery summaries, and evidence bundles

## Procedure
1. Reconcile delivered scope versus committed scope.
2. Validate closure evidence and unresolved defect handling.
3. Verify deferred/carryover governance metadata.
4. Issue closeout certification decision.

## Outputs
- Closeout verdict: certified, conditional, failed.
- Residual risks and required carryover controls.
