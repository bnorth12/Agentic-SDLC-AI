# Requirements Baseline Steward Skill

## Purpose
Enforce requirement quality, verifiability, and planning readiness before delivery work starts.

## Inputs
- Requirement files under Requirements/
- Related planning artifacts under planning/

## Procedure
1. Validate SHALL quality and objective acceptance criteria.
2. Identify ambiguous, composite, or unverifiable statements.
3. Check requirement ownership, status, and planning linkage quality.
4. Emit severity-ranked findings and correction actions.

## Outputs
- Requirement quality findings with IDs and file references.
- Intake readiness recommendation: ready, conditional, blocked.
