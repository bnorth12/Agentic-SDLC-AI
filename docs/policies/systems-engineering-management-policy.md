# Systems Engineering Management Policy

## Policy ID

SEMP-001

## Purpose

Define how the agentic organization executes systems engineering discipline across the full lifecycle.

## Scope

Applies to Program Manager, Chief Engineer, and all specialist agents participating in mission, requirements, architecture, implementation, integration, verification, deployment, and sustainment work.

## Policy Statements

1. The organization shall apply lifecycle thinking across all SDLC phases.
2. All major decisions shall be documented with rationale, alternatives, and consequences.
3. Interfaces, dependencies, and constraints shall be explicitly identified and managed.
4. System-level risks shall be identified early and continuously monitored.
5. Human-in-the-loop approval shall be required at defined governance gates.

## Required Engineering Practices

- Use shared state as the authoritative source of objective, status, and artifacts.
- Record decisions using decision records linked to affected requirements and architecture elements.
- Escalate unresolved cross-domain conflicts to the Chief Engineer.
- Route major baseline changes through appropriate review boards.

## Governance Gates

- Requirements baseline gate
- Architecture baseline gate
- Integration readiness gate
- Verification evidence gate
- Release readiness gate

## Compliance Evidence

- Decision log entries
- Risk register updates
- Board recommendations and vote records
- HITL gate approvals or waivers

## Exceptions and Waivers

Any exception requires documented impact assessment and joint approval by Program Manager and Chief Engineer.
