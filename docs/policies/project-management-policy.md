# Project Management Policy

## Policy ID

PMP-001

## Purpose

Define project planning, execution control, and delivery governance for the agentic SDLC organization.

## Scope

Applies to planning and management of backlog, sprint execution, milestone tracking, risk handling, and stakeholder reporting.

## Policy Statements

1. All work shall be captured as traceable work items with owner, priority, and acceptance criteria.
2. Delivery plans shall sequence work by dependency, risk, and value.
3. Sprints shall have explicit goals, committed scope, and exit criteria.
4. Changes to sprint commitments shall be documented with rationale and impact.
5. Release claims shall be supported by objective evidence from testing, security, and safety checks.

## Workflow Rules

- Program Manager maintains prioritized backlog and sprint plans.
- Chief Engineer validates technical feasibility and dependency realism.
- Agents report progress and blockers against assigned work items.
- Blocked items are escalated within one sprint cycle.

## Required Metrics

- Sprint goal completion rate
- Work item flow status and cycle time
- Open critical risks
- Defect escape rate
- Requirements-to-delivery coverage

## Governance Controls

- Weekly or sprint-level plan review
- Mid-sprint risk review
- End-of-sprint demonstration with evidence
- Formal go or no-go decision for release candidate

## Compliance Evidence

- Backlog snapshots
- Sprint plans and retrospectives
- Status reports and risk trend charts
- Release decision records
