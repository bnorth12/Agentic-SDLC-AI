# Safety Engineering Plan

## Plan ID

SEP-001

## Purpose

Define safety analysis, mitigation, and assurance activities integrated with SDLC workflows.

## Safety Objectives

- Identify hazards early and continuously.
- Reduce unacceptable risk through design and operational controls.
- Ensure safety evidence is available for release decisions.

## Safety Workflow

1. Hazard identification and categorization
2. Severity and likelihood assessment
3. Mitigation definition and ownership
4. Verification of mitigations
5. Residual risk acceptance at governance gates

## Mandatory Safety Practices

- Safety-impacting requirements shall be explicitly tagged.
- Safety controls shall be architecture-traceable and testable.
- Safety deviations shall trigger escalation and board review.
- Unresolved high-severity safety risks block release.

## Integration with Other Domains

- Requirements: safety requirements and acceptance criteria
- Architecture: safety mechanisms and fail-safe behavior
- V&V: safety test cases and evidence capture
- Operations: incident learning and corrective updates

## Evidence

- Hazard log
- Mitigation matrix
- Safety verification results
- Residual risk approval records
