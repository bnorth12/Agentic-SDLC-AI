# Risk Management Plan

## Plan ID

RISK-001

## Purpose

Define identification, analysis, mitigation, monitoring, and escalation of lifecycle risks.

## Risk Categories

- Technical
- Security
- Safety
- Schedule
- Resource
- External dependency

## Risk Workflow

1. Identify and register risk
2. Assess probability and impact
3. Define mitigation and contingency
4. Assign owner and due dates
5. Monitor risk status and triggers
6. Escalate unresolved high-impact risks

## Governance Rules

- Risks shall be reviewed at sprint planning and sprint review.
- High and critical risks require explicit leadership visibility.
- Risk acceptance requires documented rationale and approver.

## Metrics

- Open risk count by severity
- Mitigation completion rate
- Time-to-mitigation for high and critical risks
- Risk recurrence trend

## Evidence

- Risk register snapshots
- Mitigation status reports
- Escalation and acceptance records
