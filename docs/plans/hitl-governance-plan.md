# Human-in-the-Loop Governance Plan

## Plan ID

HITL-001

## Purpose

Define how human reviewers oversee, approve, and direct agentic workflow decisions.

## HITL Objectives

- Maintain human accountability for high-impact decisions.
- Prevent unsafe or non-compliant progression through lifecycle gates.
- Enable corrective guidance and override where necessary.

## HITL Checkpoints

- Requirements baseline approval
- Architecture baseline approval
- Major risk acceptance
- Security and safety release approval
- Final release go or no-go decision

## Reviewer Responsibilities

- Evaluate evidence completeness and policy compliance.
- Approve, reject, or conditionally approve artifacts.
- Create corrective actions with owner and due date.
- Record rationale for all gate decisions.

## Escalation and Override

- Conditional approvals require tracked closure evidence.
- Overrides require explicit rationale and accountable approver.
- Repeated policy violations trigger governance review.

## Evidence

- Approval records
- Rejection and corrective action logs
- Waiver and override history
- Gate decision audit trail
